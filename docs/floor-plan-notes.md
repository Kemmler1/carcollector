# Floor plan notes

Working notes toward `floorplan/`. Everything here is inferred from photos; nothing is measured.
Revision 3 (70 photos: 41 site photos + 29 listing photos, two of them aerials).

## What the photos establish about layout

1. **One flat roof wraps the great room.** The aerials (43 from the landward-right, 44 from the
   reef flat) show a compact grey hip (the great room) standing in the middle of a large flat
   white membrane roof. That flat roof is the pergola: the pool pavilion across the front, a
   side verandah along each flank of the great room, and the ring walkway round the courtyard.
   Two long grey hip roofs (the bedroom wings) run landward from behind the side verandahs,
   one each side of the courtyard, and a third grey-roofed wing closes the landward end.
2. **Great room** (5, 46, 47, 51, 54–56): one space under the hip. Facing the sea: lounge in
   front-left, kitchen galley behind it in the back-left corner under a flat ceiling, dining
   on the right. Open on three sides: seaward (pavilion), right (dining bi-folds onto the BBQ
   verandah) and left (lounge bi-folds plus the kitchen's side door onto the left verandah,
   photos 52 and 18). Back wall: kitchen louvre window, kitchen courtyard door, hallway beside
   the fridge (26, 50).
3. **Side verandahs** are short and sit alongside the great room, not along the wings. Each is
   ~4.5 m wide and runs from the pavilion to a bedroom's seaward bi-folds, which close its end:
   left verandah → master bedroom (2, 30, 32, 52); right verandah → bedroom 2, with the BBQ at
   the bedroom's door (1, 58, 69). Beyond those bedrooms the wings' outer walls are bare render
   with AC units and gas bottles (13, 37).
4. **Master bedroom and bedroom 2 are mirror images** (33 and 12): camera in the seaward
   bi-fold, full-height louvres on the outer wall, screened door to the courtyard walkway near
   the back, ensuite door at the back-outer corner. They sit at the seaward ends of the wings,
   i.e. landward of the verandahs and outboard of the great room's side walls.
5. **Hallway** (26, 50) runs from the great room, beside the fridge, landward onto the
   courtyard walkway; from the walkway you see straight through it to the dining table and sea.
6. **Courtyard bedrooms**: bedroom 3 (31, 48: dark four-poster, heliconia canvas, passage past
   the bed head to a further door) and the triple room (34, 49: three singles, corner room,
   louvres on two walls) open onto the walkway through bi-folds (17). Their exact positions in
   the wings are inferred: bedroom 3 at the landward end of the left wing, the triple room at
   the landward end of the right wing, with the two ensuites between.
7. **Landward wing** (36, 38–41, 35): entry porch and double screen doors on the courtyard axis,
   a raised gable over the left end room (likely the caretaker's flat the listing mentions), a
   solar hot-water panel on the right half (likely the third bathroom and laundry). The listing
   counts 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, which this plan satisfies: master + ensuite, bedroom 2 +
   ensuite, bedroom 3, triple room, bathroom 3.
8. **Pergola is continuous** and one height: pavilion → both side verandahs → courtyard ring.
   Floor is one level throughout; no steps. The courtyard ring is narrower (~1.8 m) than the
   verandahs; its corners have an open beam grid.
9. **Pool** runs parallel to the pavilion, directly off it (floor → drain → coping), centred on
   the dining half and extending right; spa nook at the right end on the pavilion side; the
   **bure** sits beyond the pool's right end, a step lower. The listing's aerials predate both
   the blue-mosaic renovation and the bure.
10. **Services**: AC units and gas bottles on both wings' outer walls (1, 13); solar HW on the
    landward wing (35).

## Unknowns — photos or measurements still needed

Priority order. Each one unblocks a part of the plan.

1. **Where exactly bedroom 3 and the triple room are** in the wings, and which courtyard door
   is which. One photo per door from the walkway.
2. **The landward wing**: is the left end really the caretaker's flat (own entrance?), and
   what is behind the right half (bathroom 3, laundry, store?).
3. **Each ensuite / bathroom**: one photo each, including which wall the door is on.
4. **Roof from a distance** at today's state: the aerials are old (pool pre-renovation, no bure).
   A drone still from the pool side would confirm the wing roofs' gablets and the far wing.
5. **Any paper**: survey, site plan, builder's drawings. Even paced-out measurements of the
   pool, the great room and one bedroom would replace most estimates above.
6. **Orientation**: compass direction of the sea view, or a map pin (the listing gives none).
7. Pool depth; whether the nook is a heated spa or a swim-out seat.
8. The walkthrough video, when it arrives: use it to check the plan room by room.

## Plan of attack

- Enough is now known to draw a **schematic plan** of the seaward half (great room, kitchen,
  master wing, pavilion, pool, bure) — doing that next in `floorplan/schematic.svg`, with
  the landward half as a dashed outline until items 1–3 arrive.
- Once any measurements arrive: scale it, add wall thicknesses (render on block: ~200 mm),
  door and window positions.
- 3D: block model from the plan + roof forms, then detail the pergola timberwork (posts,
  stepped rafter tails, lining), the great room trusses, and the bure.
