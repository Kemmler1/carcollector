# Photo log

One entry per photo in `photos/raw/`, in the order received. Each entry records where the camera
was, what is in frame, and what the photo establishes. Inferences are marked as such.

Terms used throughout:

- **Pavilion** — the large open-sided timber structure with the thatched roof, between the house
  and the pool. The main living/outdoor room.
- **Bedroom wing** — the rendered (cement-plastered, painted off-white) masonry building with the
  corrugated metal roof, attached to the pavilion.
- **Bure** — the small square thatched gazebo at the seaward corner, beside the pool.
- **Terrace** — the cream-tiled paved area around the pool, under and in front of the pavilion.

## 01 — Exterior, rear lawn, pavilion and bedroom wing
`01-exterior-rear-lawn-pavilion-and-bedroom-wing.jpg`

Camera on the dry lawn on the landward/side of the house, looking back at the building. Wide lens.

- Left two-thirds: the pavilion from outside. Dark-stained hardwood posts (~200 mm square),
  heavy beams, a low flat-pitched pergola roof with exposed rafter tails. Rafter tails have a
  stepped/notched profile.
- The pavilion's outer wall on this side is a run of **timber louvre bi-fold doors** (dark stain,
  horizontal fixed louvres in the upper panels, solid boards in the lower), folded open. Through
  them: the living room, a dark lounge, and the sea beyond — so the pavilion is open on both
  sides, landward and seaward.
- Behind the pavilion roof a second, higher **gable/dormer** is visible: cream-rendered with a
  corrugated roof. This is the living room's raised ceiling (matches the cathedral ceiling in
  photo 05).
- Right third: the bedroom wing. Off-white rendered walls, **grey corrugated metal hip roof**
  (Colorbond-type, light grey), ply-lined eaves with exposed stained rafter ends and a timber
  fascia. White PVC downpipes.
- Bedroom wing windows: dark timber-framed **louvre windows**, one large (4 louvre panels) and
  a smaller one further right.
- A pair of timber bi-fold doors on the bedroom wing stand open; a white four-poster bed with
  voile curtains is visible inside (the master bedroom, photo 12).
- Services on the right-hand wall: split-system outdoor AC unit on a plinth, two gas bottles,
  copper/PVC pipework, a tap.
- Floor: dark grey large-format tiles (~600 mm) run from inside the pavilion straight out onto a
  tiled apron, flush with the lawn. A small step/plinth of brick at the bedroom door.
- Garden: clipped green hedge against the wing's end wall, coconut palms, croton/cordyline
  shrubs. Lawn is dry, patchy, brown. Black garden hose across it.
- Sky clear, hard midday sun.

Establishes: two-building arrangement; pavilion open on both long sides; roof forms; exterior
palette; services location; floor continuity inside/out.

seaward side, bedroom wings running landward on both sides, a walkway under the same pergola
