# House reference — master description

The single document to hand to another model. Everything here is drawn from the photos in
`photos/raw/` (see `docs/photo-log.md` for the evidence per photo). Dimensions are estimates
unless stated; see `docs/floor-plan-notes.md` for the reasoning and the open questions.

Revision 2 (32 photos). Supersedes revision 1 on the pavilion roof (it is a flat pergola, not
thatched) and on the plan (it is a courtyard house).

## 1. One-paragraph summary

A single-storey tropical beachfront **courtyard house** on a flat lawn shelf a few metres above
a coral reef flat, Fijian in construction and style. A great room (lounge, dining, kitchen)
under a tall hip roof of dark-stained hardwood trusses faces the sea; bedroom wings run
landward from both ends and, with a far wing, enclose a planted **central courtyard** of
coconut palms and clipped hedges. A continuous **flat timber pergola** — dark beams, honey
board lining, square posts, stepped rafter tails — wraps the whole house: as the pool pavilion
on the seaward side, as side verandahs along the wings, and as the courtyard walkway. Every
room opens to it through dark-stained timber **louvre bi-fold doors**. In front lies a long
**royal-blue mosaic infinity pool** and, at its far end, a square **thatched bure** with a
rendered bench and daybed. Palette: chocolate-stained hardwood, honey timber boards, pale ply
ceilings, off-white render, grey-taupe tile, cream pool paving, royal blue water, natural
thatch, turquoise lagoon.

## 2. Site

- **Setting**: a low headland or shelf on an island shore. The lawn is flat and ends in a
  vegetated bank (beach hibiscus, scrub, coconut palms) dropping ~3–6 m (est.) to the reef
  flat. No beach directly below; a sandy beach lies across a shallow bay to one side.
- **Views**: open sea to the horizon on the main axis; to one side a bay, a sandy beach, a
  coconut-fringed point and green mainland hills; a distant island on the horizon. Reef flat
  pale turquoise with dark coral patches and scattered rocks; deep water a dark blue band.
- **Ground**: coarse tropical grass, dry and brown/patchy in the photos (dry season), sandy
  soil showing through. Sandy bare ground in the courtyard.
- **Vegetation**: coconut palms everywhere (many with green nuts), a dense tree line of
  casuarina and broad-leaf scrub on the landward sides, clipped hedges against the house and
  as a block in the courtyard, crotons, cordylines, a yellow-flowered shrub hedge on the
  seaward edge, potted palms and a frangipani cutting in large black glazed pots.
- **Light**: hard tropical midday sun, deep blue sky, few clouds.

Key facts of the arrangement:

- The **great room** opens on three sides: seaward (pool pavilion), right (side verandah with
  the dining table just inside and the BBQ just outside), and landward via the kitchen's own
  courtyard door and a hallway to the walkway.
- The **master bedroom** opens onto the left side verandah, which runs straight to the sea.
- A **second bedroom** opens off the right end of the great room / right verandah.
- Other bedrooms open onto the courtyard walkway (count unknown).
- The whole pergola is at one height and one floor level; no steps anywhere between rooms,
  verandahs and pavilion. Door thresholds are flush brass strips.

## 4. Structures

### 4.1 Masonry wings

- **Walls**: masonry, cement-rendered. Outside: off-white; where a wall faces a verandah it
  is instead **clad in horizontal honey timber boards** (the bedroom wing's verandah wall,
  the great room's end wall). Inside: pale sage-cream or warm cream render.
- **Roof**: hip, light grey corrugated steel. Eaves ~600 mm (est.) with pale ply soffits,
  exposed dark-stained rafter ends, dark timber fascia, white PVC gutters and round
  downpipes. The great room's roof is a taller hip above the wings' roofs.
- **Windows**: timber-framed **louvre windows** throughout — dark stain outside, honey inside
  — timber louvre blades, in banks of 1–4 panels, with black steel operating levers.
- **External doors**: timber **louvre bi-fold doors**, leaves ~500 mm, honey timber with dark
  frames; upper two-thirds fixed louvres, lower third solid boards or a mesh fly-screen
  panel; black steel hinges; brass flush threshold tracks.
- **Internal doors**: solid timber, honey or pale; some with mesh screen panels.
- **Ceilings**: pale ply boards with dark battens. Great room: tall hip with exposed trusses.
  Bedrooms: cathedral with trusses and black steel gusset plates. Kitchen and hallway: flat,
  ~2.7 m (est.), recessed downlights.
- **Floors**: one grey-taupe stone-look porcelain tile, ~600 mm, everywhere inside and on the
  verandahs and pavilion; it reads brown under warm light and grey in daylight. Bedrooms a
  slightly lighter grey; ensuite white; courtyard walkway pale grey-cream.
- **Services**: Panasonic inverter split-system AC (wall units inside, outdoor units on
  plinths on the outer walls), black 3-blade ceiling fans, LPG bottles, recessed downlights,
  white cylinder pendants.

### 4.2 Pergola (pavilion, verandahs, walkway)

- **Posts**: square dark-stained hardwood, ~200 × 200 mm (est.).
- **Beams**: heavy sawn hardwood, dark stain, galvanised/black steel plates at junctions.
  Rafters at ~900 mm (est.) centres; tails project ~600 mm past the edge beam with a
  **three-step notched end profile** that casts striped shadows.
- **Lining**: honey timber boards (~150 mm) laid across the rafters, so the underside reads
  as a flat honey ceiling with dark ribs. A few bays at the pavilion's lawn corner are left
  open to the sky.
- **Floor**: grey-taupe tile continuous with inside; a stainless **linear drain grate** runs
  along the pool-side edge of the pavilion.
- Width ~3.5 m (est.) on the verandahs and walkway, ~5 m (est.) at the pool pavilion.

### 4.3 Pool and terrace

- Pool: long rectangle, ~12 × 4 m (est.), **royal blue glass mosaic** throughout including
  the weir; **infinity/wet edge on the seaward long side**; pale stone coping. A square
  **swim-out / spa nook** with a seat cut into the terrace side at the bure end; a small tiled
  step at the other end. Bronze round skimmer lid in the paving.
- Terrace beyond the pavilion: cream/beige stone-look pavers (lighter than the house tile).

### 4.4 Bure

- Square, ~5 × 5 m (est.), open on all sides, at the far (right) end of the pool next to the
  spa nook. Round natural de-barked posts; square dark ridge beam; a grid of dark rafters and
  purlins infilled with **woven pandanus panels** (diamond weave) as the visible ceiling;
  natural palm thatch outside with a ragged eave fringe.
- Rendered masonry **L-shaped bench** (off-white, grey tile top, ~450 mm) along the seaward
  and one side edge; a raised rendered **daybed platform** with a dark brown cushion in one
  half; cream tile floor.

## 5. Rooms

### Great room
One tall space ~10 × 8 m (est.) under the hip roof: four dark trusses with black king-post
plates, ply lining, 2 black fans, 8 white cylinder pendants in a grid, a massive dark header
beam on square honey posts along the open sides. Seaward wall and right wall are bi-folds.
**Lounge** on the seaward side: two black leather sofas and two armchairs with thick round
timber log legs, blue-grey patterned cushions, a chunky timber coffee table; low dark TV
cabinet on the kitchen-end wall. **Dining** on the right: 8-seat dark timber table, slatted
chairs. Art: frangipani canvases, two carved Pacific masks flanking the hallway opening, a
mask by the kitchen. Honey board wall above the kitchen opening.

### Kitchen
A galley bay with a flat ply ceiling at the landward-left of the great room, open above bench
height. **Island** ~4 m, dark vertical-board base, sand stone top with raised bar ledge, 1.5
bowl stainless sink, mixer, dishwasher; 4 dark square bar stools on the lounge side. **Back
bench**: gas hob, under-bench oven, kettle, toaster, rice cooker; 4-panel louvre window over;
cream render splashback. Tall dark pantry bank in a nib; LG stainless fridge in a nib with a
cupboard over. A **desk with drawers and cane chair** at the end of the galley. Water cooler.
Own bi-fold door to the courtyard side.

### Hallway
From the great room, beside the fridge, landward to the courtyard walkway. Louvre doors off it.

### Master bedroom (left wing)
Cathedral ply ceiling, dark trusses, black fan, Panasonic wall AC. King four-poster, black
square posts, white voile tied back, white linen; bird-of-paradise or frangipani canvas; black
wall reading lights; grey upholstered armchair; dark timber towel cabinet; timber trunk. Louvre
windows on the verandah wall. Bi-folds open onto the left verandah with the sea straight ahead.
Door to an ensuite (white tile, mirror, seaward opening). Screened door to the next room.

### Second bedroom (right end)
Off the right end of the great room. Four-poster with dark posts, red heliconia canvas, black
reading lights, tropical-print bedside table, cream render, cathedral ply ceiling. Short tiled
passage past the bed head to a pale timber door (ensuite, unconfirmed). Louvre windows to lawn.

### Courtyard bedrooms (unseen inside)
Doors and louvre windows onto the walkway; paintings on the walls outside each.

### Ensuites
One glimpsed: white large tile, mirror. Others unknown.
