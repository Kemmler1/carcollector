# House reference — master description

The single document to hand to another model. Everything here is drawn from the photos in
`photos/raw/` (see `docs/photo-log.md` for the evidence per photo). Dimensions are estimates
unless stated; see `docs/floor-plan-notes.md` for the reasoning and the open questions.

## 1. One-paragraph summary

A single-storey tropical beachfront house on a flat lawn shelf a few metres above a coral reef
flat, Fijian in construction and style. It is three connected pieces: (1) a **rendered masonry
bedroom/living wing** with an off-white finish and a light grey corrugated metal hip roof;
(2) a large **open-air timber pavilion** along the wing's seaward face, half under a flat timber
pergola and half under a thatched hip roof with woven pandanus ceiling panels, floored in
cream stone tile; and (3) a **royal-blue mosaic infinity pool** on the pavilion's seaward side
with a small square **thatched bure** at its far corner. Every room opens to the pavilion
through dark-stained timber louvre bi-fold doors. The palette is dark chocolate-stained
hardwood, honey-coloured timber boards, pale ply ceilings, off-white render, cream and grey
tiles, royal blue pool, natural thatch, against turquoise reef and coconut palms.

## 2. Site

- **Setting**: a low headland or shelf on an island shore. The lawn is flat and ends in a
  vegetated bank (beach hibiscus, scrub, coconut palms) dropping ~3–6 m (est.) to the reef
  flat. No beach directly below; a sandy beach lies across a shallow bay to one side.
- **Views**: open sea to the horizon on the main axis; to one side, a bay, a sandy beach, a
  coconut-fringed point and green mainland hills; a distant island on the horizon. Reef flat
  pale turquoise with dark coral patches and scattered rocks; deep water a dark blue band.
- **Ground**: lawn is coarse tropical grass, dry and brown/patchy in the photos (dry season),
  sandy soil showing through.
- **Vegetation**: coconut palms everywhere (many with green nuts), a dense tree line of
  casuarina and broad-leaf scrub on the landward sides, clipped green hedge against the house,
  crotons, cordylines, a yellow-flowered shrub hedge on the seaward edge, potted palms and a
  frangipani cutting in large black glazed pots.
- **Light**: hard tropical midday sun, deep blue sky, few clouds.

## 3. Buildings and arrangement

Working orientation (unconfirmed): the house wing is on the landward side; the pavilion is in
front of it; the pool is in front of the pavilion; the bure is at the pool's far seaward corner.

Note: the master bedroom opens to a **dark grey tiled apron and the lawn** at the left end of
the pavilion; the rest of the pavilion floor is cream tile. The BBQ sits at the opposite
(right) landward corner.

### 3.1 Bedroom / living wing

- **Walls**: concrete block or masonry, cement-rendered, painted off-white/warm white outside;
  pale sage-cream inside.
- **Roof**: hip, light grey corrugated steel (Colorbond-type). Eaves ~600 mm (est.) with pale
  ply soffits, exposed dark-stained rafter ends, dark timber fascia, white PVC gutters and
  round downpipes. A raised gable/dormer over the living room.
- **Windows**: timber-framed **louvre windows** throughout, dark stain, timber louvre blades
  (not glass), in banks of 1–4 panels. Internal timber trim.
- **External doors**: dark-stained timber **louvre bi-fold doors**, each leaf ~500 mm wide,
  upper two-thirds fixed horizontal louvres, lower third solid vertical boards; brass/bronze
  hardware. One bedroom leaf is a lighter honey/orange timber.
- **Internal doors**: solid timber, honey colour, some with a woven/mesh fly-screen panel.
- **Ceilings**: pale plywood sheets with dark cover battens. Living room is cathedral with
  exposed dark-stained rafters; bedroom is cathedral with dark trusses and black steel gusset
  plates; kitchen is a flat lower ceiling with recessed downlights.
- **Floors**: inside, brown/taupe large-format ceramic tile (~450–600 mm). Bedroom: light grey
  tile. Ensuite: white large tile.
- **Services**: split-system AC (wall units inside, outdoor units on plinths against the
  landward wall), ceiling fans, LPG bottles for the hob, downlights, white cylinder pendants.

### 3.2 Pavilion

- **Posts**: square dark-stained hardwood, ~200×200 mm (est.), on the tile.
- **Beams**: heavy sawn hardwood, dark stain, with galvanised/black steel plate connectors at
  junctions. Rafter tails project past the beams with a **stepped, notched end profile** (three
  steps) — a distinctive detail; they cast striped shadows on the terrace.
- **Roof A — flat pergola** (house side): open beam grid; bays lined above with honey timber
  boards running across the beams; some bays left open to sky.
- **Roof B — thatched hip** (pool side): dark rafters and purlins forming a square grid;
  **woven pandanus panels** with a diamond/chevron weave as the visible ceiling; natural palm
  thatch outside with a ragged fringe at the eave.
- **Floor**: cream/beige stone-look tile; a **linear stainless drain grate** runs along the
  house wall; dark grey tile at the bedroom apron.
- **Furniture**: two carved hardwood rocking chairs (curved arms, slatted); a low timber chest
  coffee table; five grey-beige outdoor bean-bag loungers in a row along the pool; two white
  plastic sun loungers; a stainless 4-burner hooded BBQ; large black glazed pots with palms.

### 3.3 Pool

- Long rectangle, ~12 × 4 m (est.). **Royal blue glass mosaic** throughout, including the
  weir. **Infinity/wet edge** on the seaward long side, spilling to a trough below. Pale stone
  coping. A small tiled step at one end and a square **swim-out / spa nook** with seat cut into
  the terrace side near the bure. Bronze round skimmer lid in the paving.

### 3.4 Bure

- Square, ~3.5 × 3.5 m (est.), open on all sides. Four round natural de-barked posts; square
  dark ridge beam; radial sawn rafters; battens; natural thatch hip roof.
- Rendered masonry **L-shaped bench** (off-white, grey tile top) along the seaward and one side
  edge; a raised rendered **daybed platform** with a dark brown cushion in one half; cream tile
  floor elsewhere.

## 4. Rooms seen so far

### Living room
Tall cathedral space. Brown tile floor. Pale render walls with dark timber cornice. Two large
frangipani-on-green canvases, two carved Pacific wooden masks flanking the kitchen opening.
Honey timber-boarded gable wall above the kitchen. Island bar with vertical honey board front,
sand stone top, four dark square bar stools. Desk and cane chair on one side, dark sideboard
on the other. Two white cylinder pendants. Opens through louvre bi-folds to the pavilion.

### Kitchen
A lower bay off the living room, framed by two square honey-timber posts. Dark-stained
vertical-board cabinets with long stainless bar pulls; sand/beige stone benchtops; stainless
4-burner gas hob, under-bench oven, LG stainless fridge; island with brown composite sink.
Four-panel timber louvre window above the back bench; cream render splashback; tall pantry
cupboards in nibs either side. Small white-flower prints.

### Master bedroom
Cathedral ply ceiling, dark trusses, ceiling fan. King black four-poster bed with white voile
and white linen; frangipani canvas over the bed; wall reading lights; timber trunk at the foot.
Louvre windows, wall AC. Light grey tile. Opens to the pavilion/lawn via louvre bi-folds. Door
to an ensuite (white tile, mirror, seaward opening). Screened door to the adjoining room.

### Ensuite (glimpsed only)
White large tiles, mirror. Opens seaward.

### Other rooms
At least one more room beyond the living room on the right, reached by more bi-fold doors.
Not yet photographed.
